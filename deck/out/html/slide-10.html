G1 cleans the dirtiest first.

Garbage-First GC — the default since JDK 9. Regions, not generations-in-concrete.

G1 in 20 seconds
  • G1 = Garbage-First. Heap split into equal regions.
  • A region can be Eden, Survivor, Old or Humongous.
  • G1 collects the regions with the most garbage first.
  • Goal: big reclamation within a pause-time target.

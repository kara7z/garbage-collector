Mark. Sweep. Compact.

The classic 3-step model behind (almost) every collector — find the living, free the dead, tidy up.

The cycle, every time
1
MARK — find who is alive
Trace from GC Roots and tag every reachable object.
2
SWEEP — reclaim the rest
Free memory of unreachable objects. Nothing points to them.
3
COMPACT — defragment
Slide survivors together so free space is one clean block.
